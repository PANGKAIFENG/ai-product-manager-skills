# Decision Research Mode Routing

Use this reference after the Startup Gate to keep `decision-research` focused on the user's real decision, not on a generic research dump.

## Routing Questions

1. What decision will change after this research?
2. Is the object a technology, platform, product direction, business model, competitor move, or market narrative?
3. What would make the current leading hypothesis false?
4. What evidence is available publicly, and what remains local or hidden?
5. Is a final recommendation required now, or should the output become a Candidate Backlog for later?

## Handoff Rules

- Use `research-topic-compiler` when the user wants systematic learning, a candidate pool, a research project, or long-running radar.
- Use `competitive-analysis` when the primary job is collecting competitor evidence and translating it into a Product Decision Brief.
- Use `ai-collaboration-calibration` when the user is describing a fuzzy feeling, phenomenon, or raw solution without a stable decision question.
- Use `grill-me` when the user already has a solution and wants interactive pressure testing rather than evidence research.

## Completion Test

The result should let the user answer: "What should we choose, what should we exclude, under which assumptions, and what signal would overturn the answer?"
