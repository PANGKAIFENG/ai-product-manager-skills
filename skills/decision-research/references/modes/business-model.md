# Business Model Mode

Use when the decision is pricing, packaging, free vs paid, basic vs pro, team vs enterprise, add-on structure, or monetization sequence.

## Required Checks

- Buyer and user may differ; identify both.
- Paid trigger: which moment creates willingness to pay?
- Packaging axis: usage, seats, workflow depth, governance, integrations, support, data volume, automation, collaboration, or risk reduction.
- Competitive references: pricing pages, plan comparison, enterprise gates, investor materials, changelog, customer stories.
- Fairness and adoption: avoid pricing that punishes activation before value is felt.

## Decision Output

Include:

- Recommended packaging axis.
- What belongs in base vs paid tiers.
- What should remain unpriced until value is proven.
- Evidence and assumptions.
- Overturn condition, such as customer segment, adoption signal, or sales motion changing.
