# Product Strategy Mode

Use when the decision is product direction, positioning, user group, workflow focus, differentiation, category narrative, or roadmap priority.

## Top-Down Order

1. Category narrative: what value do leading products claim?
2. User role and JTBD: who has the pain and what job is urgent?
3. Workflow: where does the product enter the user's real work?
4. Capability system: what abilities are required to win that workflow?
5. Implementation: what is the smallest credible version?
6. Packaging: how would users understand, buy, or adopt it?

## Required Checks

- Do not start from a feature list.
- Translate user-provided solution words into product-layer questions.
- Separate validated market signals from inference.
- Include what to ignore or postpone.
- Tie every recommendation to a user job or business consequence.

## Decision Output

Include:

- Recommended positioning or product move.
- What not to build now.
- Evidence strength and weak signals.
- Most important unknown.
- A small validation action before roadmap commitment.
