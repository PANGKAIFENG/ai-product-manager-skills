Use this template when `product-research` must turn competitor or alternative
evidence into decision input. It may recommend `Copy`, `Adapt`, `Avoid`,
`Monitor`, or `Validate first`; choosing a final candidate remains the
responsibility of `decision-research`.

Quality bar:

- The direction is falsifiable and does not masquerade as final candidate selection.
- Important observations change a product judgment, validation step, or explicit non-goal.
- At least one item says what not to copy.
- Weak evidence remains visibly weak.
- Browser observations include access dates and screenshot paths or locatable notes.
